CliCR, in your browser

Ask a question about Flask's source code. The page finds the code that answers it with the same hybrid search as the CliCR command-line tool: a meaning search (embeddings), a keyword search (BM25) and a merge of the two (reciprocal-rank fusion). Everything runs on your device; nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Ask

The first search downloads the embedding model Xenova/bge-small-en-v1.5 from huggingface.co (about 67 MB; Hugging Face serves the large file from its CDN, cdn.hf.co), and the Transformers.js library with its ONNX runtime from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Once only; your browser keeps them. Your question never leaves the page.

Answer

How the ranking was built

Every candidate from either search. Fused score = sum of 1 / (60 + rank) over the lists that hold the chunk. The top 6 by fused score are shown; the code-graph step may add definitions of functions they call.
