description: "Built at the Claude Impact Lab hackathon (UCI × SoCal Affordable Housing, November 7, 2026). Orange County cities each write their own rules for backyard homes (ADUs), duplexes and lot splits, and some ask for things state law says a city cannot require. Pick what you're building and Fast Track reads the city's rules next to state law and the state housing department's letters to that city. Each clash is shown in plain words with the exact text, the source and the date it was checked; for example, 6 of Dana Point's 9 duplex rules clash with state law. It then drafts a reply you edit and send yourself, and shows the city's legal deadline to answer. A prototype only: not legal advice, and nothing you type leaves the page."
