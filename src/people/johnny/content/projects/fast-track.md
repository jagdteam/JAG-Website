description: "Built in one day at the Claude Impact Lab hackathon (UCI × SoCal Affordable Housing, November 7, 2026). Orange County cities each write their own checklist for backyard homes (ADUs), and some lines ask for things state law says a city cannot require. Fast Track marks each line Allowed, Not allowed, Old law number or Not checked, shows the state law and the state housing department's letter to that city with the date it was checked, and drafts a reply the applicant edits and sends themselves. A prototype only: not legal advice, and it never sends anything."
